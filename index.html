Will this scan in real life?

Verify confirms the code encodes correctly — it can't guarantee a phone will read it in the wild, since that depends on how big and how far away the code ends up being printed or shown. Rule of thumb:

minimum size ≈ scanning distance ÷ 10

Use case Scan distance Minimum size
Business card / handout ~1 ft (arm's length) 1 in (2.5 cm)
Flyer or poster ~3 ft 3.5 in (9 cm)
Storefront sign ~10 ft 12 in (30 cm)
Projected slide, back row ~20 ft 24 in (60 cm) on-screen

Longer URLs pack in more detail and need extra size to stay scannable — shortening the link is usually the easiest fix. Sharing a code on a screen for someone to scan off another screen (e.g. a slide deck) is its own risk — keep those codes short, and consider adding the link as a backup.
